# Insights

- [Hypothesis board](hypotheses.md) — proposed, testing, confirmed, or refuted.

Runtime-generated candidates must include evidence refs, sample count,
confidence, and a suggested action. They are not Lessons or Skills until an
external agent/human promotes them through the gated write path.
